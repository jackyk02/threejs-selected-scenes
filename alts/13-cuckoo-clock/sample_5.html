THE BLACK FOREST CHALET

Hand-carved leaves, hunting birds and a deer skull — an endless-chain cuckoo clock with a visible eight-day going train.

idle · 60 fps

Drag the minute hand. Press F for ×60. Click the cuckoo door. Pull either chain downward to rewind its pine-cone weight.

S C H W A R Z W A L D  ·  E S C A P E M E N T
Watch for 7:00. The door flings open, the bird calls seven times, dancers whirl and the waterwheel turns. The cut-away right cheek shows the meshing gear train locked to one revolution of the minute wheel per clock hour.
